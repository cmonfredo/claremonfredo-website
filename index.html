Research Assistant Professor, University of Rochester

Clare Monfredo is a cellist and researcher. Her work examines interpretation and individuality in classical music — how performers negotiate the constraints and freedoms of this tradition.

Clare performs across the United States and internationally, while also teaching and conducting musical scholarship. She is the co-founder and co-director of DownEast New Music, a contemporary music ensemble that performs in coastal Maine and commissions new works by today’s leading composers.

performance

Clare has performed across the United States and Europe with ensembles including Ensemble Intercontemporain, A Far Cry, and International Contemporary Ensemble, at venues from Carnegie Hall to the Hamburg Elbphilharmonie.
