Performance Research & Writing About & CV DownEast New Music

performance

As a cellist, Clare has performed stylistically wide-ranging music across the United States and Europe, including with Ensemble Intercontemporain, A Far Cry, Lucerne Contemporary Orchestra, International Contemporary Ensemble, Heartbeat Opera, and Wet Ink Ensemble; alongside jazz musicians including Ron Carter and Jon Batiste; and in collaboration with artists including Nene Humphrey and Miya Masaoka.

A dedicated chamber musician, Clare is a member of the New York-based chamber group Sonora Collective and is co-founder and co-artistic director of DownEast New Music, Maine’s first chamber music ensemble focused exclusively on contemporary music.
